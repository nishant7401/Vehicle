Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our fleet and services.

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?

We typically respond within 2 hours during business hours. For urgent matters like roadside assistance, call our 24/7 hotline at +977 9876 65521.

Can I book a vehicle for a Himalayan trip?

Absolutely! Our Mahindra Thar ROXX and Scorpio-N 4×4 jeeps are perfect for Mustang, Rara, and Dolpo trips. Prices start at रू 11,000/day with driver.

Do you provide vehicles with a driver?

Yes! We offer both self-drive and chauffeur-driven options. Driver charges start at रू 2,000/day for city trips and vary for long-distance travel.

What documents do I need to rent a vehicle?

For self-drive: valid driving licence, citizenship/passport copy, and a security deposit. For chauffeur-driven: just your ID. Foreign tourists need an International Driving Permit.

Do you offer corporate or long-term rentals?

Yes! We offer discounted monthly and corporate packages. Contact us for a custom quote — usually 20–35% off standard daily rates for 30+ days.

Is there a mileage limit on rentals?

Most city rentals come with unlimited mileage. For out-of-valley trips, we offer generous daily limits (200–300 km). Extra km is charged at रू 25–40/km.
